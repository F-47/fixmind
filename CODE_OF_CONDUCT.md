# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Fixmind contributors and community members must communicate respectfully, discuss ideas rather than attack people, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to Fixmind repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, project-managed community spaces, and private communication that concerns participation in the project.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Email `support@fixmind.dev` with the relevant links or context and a safe way to contact you. Do not include unrelated personal information.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban a participant when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled privately to the extent reasonably possible; confidentiality cannot be promised when disclosure is required to address immediate safety or legal obligations.

## Good-faith enforcement

Enforcement decisions will consider the behavior, its impact, prior warnings, and whether the participant takes responsibility and changes their conduct. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports violate this policy.
